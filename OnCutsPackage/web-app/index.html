CampusCut - The Booking App for Campus Barbers

Fair prices for students, great earnings for barbers. Book haircuts on campus with transparent pricing and instant payments.

Features

  • Book haircuts with campus barbers
  • Set your availability and pricing
  • Secure payments via Stripe
  • Real-time messaging

Google Calendar Integration

Barbers can optionally connect their Google Calendar to prevent double-bookings. CampusCut reads your calendar's busy times (like classes and exams) and automatically blocks those slots so customers can't book during them. We only see when you're busy, not the details of your events. This feature helps student barbers balance their class schedule with their barbering business.
